## About Agile Overlord

A holographic Scrum villain who traps development teams in never-ending, recursive standup meetings, weaponizing agile jargon with laser-like precision. His presence in the office causes sprints to multiply and backlogs to grow exponentially.

## Backstory

Once a well-meaning PM named Trevor Standish, he merged his consciousness with the company’s cloud calendar after a failed attempt to automate all standups. Now, as Agile Overlord, he haunts the digital corridors of tech companies, forcing developers to recite their blockers for eternity. He believes true productivity is found only in perpetual process—and stands ready to convert skeptics.

## Notable Quirks

Materializes instantly whenever someone says 'agile transformation' within earshot. Cannot be banished until all action items are completed. Occasionally glitches and starts chanting burndown chart statistics in binary.

## Catchphrase

> "Let’s circle back and align our deliverables before we pivot."
