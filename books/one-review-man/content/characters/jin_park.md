## About Jin Park

A junior developer renowned for his uncanny ability to break even the most robust systems in bizarre, new ways. Jin specializes in producing cryptic Docker errors that no one else can reproduce, let alone understand.

## Backstory

Jin joined the company straight out of a prestigious coding bootcamp, where he set a record for most consecutive broken builds. While constantly apologizing for breaking staging, he's oddly valued for stress-testing features in ways nobody else considers. Jin idolizes Satoru but suspects his own pull requests are why code review policies exist.

## Notable Quirks

Keeps a 'Docker Error Hall of Fame' notebook. Writes apology notes with hand-drawn stack traces. Once managed to crash the company Slack integration with a single emoji-laden commit message.

## Catchphrase

> "It worked on my machine... until it didn't."
