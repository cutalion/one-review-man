## About Hiroshi Tanaka

An intimidating technical interviewer notorious for crushing the confidence of developers during pair programming interviews. Known widely as 'The Interview Gauntlet', he tests candidates with increasingly difficult coding challenges, often leaving seasoned developers doubting their own skills.

## Backstory

Once a promising junior developer who froze during a critical technical interview early in his career, Hiroshi vowed never again to be caught off-guard. He obsessively trained, becoming an unrivaled coding expert and eventually assuming the role of a feared interviewer. Now, he lives to challenge aspirants, believing true strength emerges only when one faces their coding fears head-on.

## Notable Quirks

Always wears dark sunglasses indoors during interviews; dramatically taps the 'Enter' key when running test cases; carries a stress ball shaped like a semicolon.

## Catchphrase

> "Compile your courage, or face runtime despair!"
