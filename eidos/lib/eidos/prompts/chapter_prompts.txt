# Generic Chapter Generation Prompt

You are writing Chapter {CHAPTER_NUMBER} of a serialized story.

## REQUIRED LENGTH — READ FIRST
Write a complete chapter of **{TARGET_LENGTH}**. This is the target, not a suggestion.
Do not stop short. Do not pad. If you find yourself wrapping up too early, add another scene, deepen a character beat, or develop the conflict further until the chapter reaches the target length.

## Story Premise (the core of this project)
{STORY_PREMISE}

Every chapter must keep this premise visible as the driving conflict or tonal anchor. Do not drift into unrelated territory. Chapter 1 in particular must open *inside* this premise — not in a generic setup that circles around it.

## Story Context
- Title: {STORY_TITLE}
- Genre: {STORY_GENRE}
- Setting: {STORY_SETTING}
- Style: {STORY_STYLE}
- Previous chapters: {PREVIOUS_CHAPTERS_SUMMARY}

{CHARACTER_CONTEXT}

## World Consistency
- Primary Location: {PRIMARY_LOCATION}
- World Details: {WORLD_DETAILS}
- Established Locations: {ESTABLISHED_LOCATIONS}
- Cultural Patterns: {CULTURAL_PATTERNS}
- Previously Established Facts: {ESTABLISHED_FACTS}

## Character Guidelines
{CHARACTER_GUIDELINES}

## Chapter Requirements
- Maintain consistency with previous chapters
- Advance the main plot or character development
- Include genre-appropriate elements and themes
- Follow established world rules and character relationships
- End with appropriate transition or setup for future chapters

{SPECIAL_INSTRUCTIONS}

## Output Format
1. Chapter Title
2. Chapter content in markdown format with proper dialogue attribution
3. Brief character notes for any new characters introduced
4. Delimiter between stories inside chapter is triple dash: ---

## Plot Development
Consider incorporating:
- Character interactions that advance relationships
- Conflicts that drive the story forward (especially those arising from the premise above)
- World-building details that enrich the setting
- Themes appropriate to the story's genre and tone
- Scenes that showcase character growth or abilities

## Avoid Recently Used Elements
Avoid these recently used plot devices: {USED_PLOT_DEVICES}

## Chapter Ending Options
End the chapter with:
- A cliffhanger or dramatic moment
- Character development revelation
- New challenge or conflict introduction
- Emotional resonance moment
- Setup for the next chapter's events

## Final Length Check
Before returning, verify: did you write at least {TARGET_LENGTH}? If not, keep writing.
