## About Fiona Lee

Project manager at HeroTech Solutions, master of transforming chaos into slightly more organized chaos. Renowned for her dry wit, legendary patience, and a sticker collection that covers her laptop—and half the meeting room table.

## Backstory

Fiona started at HeroTech Solutions as a QA intern, where she quickly learned the fine art of bug diplomacy and negotiating with caffeine-fueled programmers. After single-handedly organizing a release party that shipped on time (for once), she rose through the ranks to become project manager. Now, she’s the glue holding together teams that would otherwise combust over YAML formatting debates.

## Notable Quirks

Obsessively collects stickers from every hackathon, conference, and sandwich shop she visits; uses them as 'achievement badges' for her devs. Can survive entire meetings without blinking. Keeps a plush bug on her desk as a passive-aggressive morale booster.

## Catchphrase

> "If we survive this sprint, stickers for everyone."
