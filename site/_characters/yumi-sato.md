## About Yumi Sato

Yumi Sato is the hyper-organized Product Manager at {COMPANY_NAME} who approaches every quarter like a boss raid against cross-team misalignment. She lives inside slide decks, roadmaps, and prioritization matrices, wielding Gantt charts and OKRs like legendary weapons. In the chaos of shifting requirements and surprise production bugs, Yumi is the rare PM who can freeze a room with a single, color-coded timeline and make even Senior Engineers feel like under-leveled party members who forgot to read the quest log.
