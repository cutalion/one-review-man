## About VP of Growth

The VP of Growth, real name Mira Kandel, is a metrics-fixated executive who sees the entire engineering org as one massive A/B test. She treats products, processes, and even interpersonal relationships as experiments to be optimized for engagement, retention, or some vaguely defined 'activation.' Her greatest joy is green numbers on a dashboard; her second greatest joy is launching barely tested features and calling production outages 'learning opportunities.' In the world of One Review Man, she behaves like a high-ranking hero of experimentation, constantly pushing teams into reckless hackathons and 'growth sprints' that leave QA in tears and infrastructure on fire.
